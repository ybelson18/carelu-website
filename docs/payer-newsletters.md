# Payer provider newsletter / bulletin archives

Every payer and state Medicaid program in the directory, with the official page that **lists** its provider newsletters, bulletins or notices (an archive index, not a single issue). Each index is cited in the relevant guide's `sources` array, so `scripts/payer-watch/watch.mjs` hashes it weekly and alerts when a new issue appears.

Use this list to subscribe the **payer-news@carelu.com** inbox: the *Email sign-up* column is the subscribe page where one exists. *Login* = the archive (or the newsletter itself) sits behind a provider portal login.

Compiled 2026-10-18. 319 distinct lists covering 304 of 331 payer guides (49 of 51 state Medicaid programs; no public archive found for Alaska Medicaid and New Hampshire Medicaid). 110 lists have an email sign-up; 1 public list needs a provider login to read, and 7 payers keep provider news only inside a login-gated portal (listed at the end).

**Per-state Aetna, Cigna and UnitedHealthcare commercial guides** (`aetna-<state>`, `cigna-<state>`, `unitedhealthcare-<state>`) publish no state newsletter; they are covered by the national indexes on the `aetna`, `cigna` and `unitedhealthcare-optum` guides (first rows).

**Watcher caveats.** Several indexes render their list in the browser (Anthem/Wellpoint/Healthy Blue providernews SPAs, Centene `newsroom.html` pages, Highmark, Regence, Blue Cross NC) or block bots (mass.gov, maine.gov, tn.gov, aetnabetterhealth.com, CT BHP, Carelon PA). A plain page hash may miss new items or fail quietly there; read them through `https://r.jina.ai/<url>` per the SOURCE ACCESS table in `docs/monthly-refresh.md`. Year-scoped pages (Michigan 2026 bulletins, NCTracks 2026 announcements, NY Medicaid Update 2026, DMAS memo-2026, HCA provider-alerts-2026, BCBSTX Medicaid /news/2026, Utah MIB 2026 folder, SilverSummit 2026 notifications, CareFirst BlueLink 2026) need the next year's URL each January.
